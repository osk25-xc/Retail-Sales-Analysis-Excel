# Excel Retail Dashboard Validation Checklist

- Confirm the source range includes all records and headers.
- Check date, currency, quantity, and percentage data types.
- Reconcile total sales and profit to source data.
- Verify calculated columns on the first, middle, and last rows.
- Refresh all PivotTables after source data changes.
- Test every slicer and confirm KPI cards and charts respond as intended.
- Use clear titles, units, and date ranges on charts.
- Check formulas for divide-by-zero cases and missing values.
- State the reporting period and clearly label sample or synthetic data.
- Capture screenshots only from the actual workbook after refresh.
